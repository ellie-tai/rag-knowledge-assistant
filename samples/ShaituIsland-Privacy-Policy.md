# Shaitu Island Privacy Policy

> This document is a **fictional sample** written for this project. The company, the product and all content are not real and have no legal effect.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

### Article 1 Introduction
Shaitu Island Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us" or "the Company") values your privacy. This Policy explains how we collect, process, use and protect your personal data when we provide the Shaitu Island service (the "Service").

### Article 2 Categories of Personal Data We Collect
We collect the following personal data:
1. Account data: mobile phone number, email address, nickname, date of birth, gender and profile picture.
2. User content: photos, videos, text, comments and direct messages you post.
3. Photo metadata: time of capture, capturing device and capture location (GPS coordinates).
4. Contacts: names and phone numbers in your phone's address book, when you turn on the "Find Friends" feature.
5. Device and usage data: device model, operating system version, IP address, browsing and interaction history, and time spent.
6. Payment data: transaction records for Plus membership subscriptions. Full credit card numbers are handled by our payment provider and are not stored by us.

### Article 3 Purposes of Collection
We collect personal data for the following purposes:
1. To provide, maintain and improve the Service.
2. To recommend people you may know and content you may be interested in.
3. To process subscriptions and payments.
4. For statistical analysis, product development and the training of artificial intelligence models.
5. To provide personalized advertising and marketing information.
6. To comply with legal obligations.

### Article 4 Facial Recognition and Auto-Tagging
The "Auto-Tagging" feature is turned on by default. It analyzes facial features in the photos you upload and suggests other users who may appear in them. You may turn this feature off under "Settings > Privacy". Facial feature data created before you turn it off will be deleted within 30 days after you turn it off.

### Article 5 Personalized Advertising and Tracking
Personalized advertising is turned on by default. We analyze your interests based on your browsing history, interaction history and location data, and display ads accordingly. We may also collect information about your activities outside the Service through tracking technologies on our partners' websites and apps. You may turn off personalized advertising under "Settings > Privacy"; you will still see ads, but they will no longer be tailored to your personal data.

### Article 6 Sharing with Third Parties
We may provide personal data to third parties in the following cases:
1. Service providers: companies that help us operate, such as cloud hosting, data analytics and content moderation providers.
2. Advertising partners: your interest categories and ad interaction records, for ad delivery and performance measurement.
3. Affiliates: our affiliated companies, for service integration and marketing.
4. Business transfers: in the event of a merger, acquisition or asset transfer, personal data may be transferred to the acquiring party.
5. When required by law or by a competent authority.

### Article 7 International Transfers
Our cloud service providers may store data on servers outside Taiwan. You agree that your personal data may be transferred abroad for processing and storage.

### Article 8 Data Retention Periods
We retain each category of data as shown below. After the retention period ends, we delete the data or process it so that it can no longer identify you.

| Data category | Retention period | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | While the account exists, and 30 days after account deletion | The account can be restored within 30 days |
| Photos, videos and posts | While the account exists, and 90 days after deletion | Backups are removed gradually within 90 days; content already shared by others is excluded |
| Direct messages | While the account exists, and 90 days after account deletion | Copies in the other party's account are not deleted when you delete your account |
| Facial feature data | Until 30 days after Auto-Tagging is turned off | See Article 4 |
| Location data | 2 years after collection | De-identified statistics may be kept permanently |
| Browsing and interaction records | 3 years after collection | Used for recommendation and ad analysis |
| Payment and transaction records | 7 years after the transaction | Kept under tax and accounting laws |

### Article 9 Your Rights
You may exercise the following rights regarding your personal data:
1. To inquire about or review your data.
2. To request a copy.
3. To request supplementation or correction.
4. To request that we stop collecting, processing or using your data.
5. To request deletion.

We may charge a reasonable fee for handling your requests. We may refuse a deletion request where the data is necessary for our business or required by law. Data that has already been used to train artificial intelligence models cannot be removed from models that have already been trained.

### Article 10 Data Security
We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, such as encryption in transit and access control. However, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We are not liable for data breaches that are not caused by our intentional misconduct or gross negligence.

### Article 11 Changes to This Policy
We may amend this Policy. Amendments take effect when they are published in the Service. For material changes, we will notify you through an in-app notice.

### Article 12 Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Policy, or wish to exercise your rights under Article 9, please contact us at privacy@example.com.
